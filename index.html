The Waylock field guide

Know the route before you go

By the Waylock team · Last updated: 9 November 2026

You may need one delivery stop to stay in place, or a clear choice after a wrong turn. These guides explain Waylock's planned route controls and where its Android release stands.

03 · HELD
Release status

Is Waylock available yet?

Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet. These guides change when a release does. Device checks on the route controls remain open, so until those checks pass, these guides describe planned controls rather than steps you can rely on.

01 / Planning

Which route control do you want to understand?

Start with route controls. Three choices are planned:

01

Hold a delivery stop

Keep a chosen stop in its position while reviewing an estimated order for the other stops.

Read guide ↗02

Choose a distance range

Look for a matching trip stop within a range measured from the origin or a previous stop.

Read guide ↗03

Respond to a wrong turn

Review your chosen route as an overlay while Google Navigation SDK may reroute guidance, then choose a way back or a new route when stopped.

Read guide ↗

The controls still need device and released-app verification. Check an estimated order and its constraints before setting off. Times are estimates.

02 / Account

Where are account and data details?

Account and data links to Waylock's Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and account deletion page. The deletion flow requires you to sign in and confirm with Google again, whether you use the website after uninstalling or the Android app. A requested deletion waits 7 days, and you can cancel it during that wait. Both paths still need live verification. Plans are bought on the website through Polar, not in the app.
