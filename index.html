The Waylock field guide

Know the route before you go.

By the Waylock team · Reviewed 22 October 2026

You may need one delivery stop to stay in place, or a clear choice after a wrong turn. These guides explain Waylock's planned route controls and where its Android release stands.

Release status

Is Waylock available yet?

Waylock is in development for Android. It is not listed on Google Play yet. Signed-app device checks, service checks and Play submission remain open, so these guides describe planned controls rather than steps you can use in a released app today.

01 / Planning

Which route control do you want to understand?

Start with route controls. Three choices are planned. The controls still need device and released-app verification. Check an estimated order and its constraints before setting off.

01

Hold a delivery stop

Keep a chosen stop in its position while reviewing an estimated order for the other stops.

Choose a distance range

Look for a matching trip stop within a range measured from the origin or a previous stop.

After a wrong turn

Review your chosen route as an overlay while Google Navigation SDK may reroute guidance, then choose a way back or a new route when stopped.

02 / Account

Where are account and data details?

The account and data guide links to Waylock's legal pages. The planned deletion flow requires you to sign in and confirm with Google again, whether you use the website after uninstalling or the Android app. Both paths still need live verification.
