Where can I find account and data information?

By the Waylock team · Last updated: 7 November 2026

The Privacy Policy explains the information Waylock handles. The Terms of Service explain the service conditions.

How would I delete my account after uninstalling the app?​

The account-deletion page is up, and its flow has not yet passed a live deletion test. It is a signed-in self-service path that asks you to use the Google account linked to Waylock, confirm that sign-in again and explicitly confirm deletion. Deletion then waits 7 days. Sign-in stops during that wait, and you can cancel on the same page or on Account. Removal comes on or soon after the date shown, in your time zone. The website and in-app paths need verification; an uncertain response does not prove that deletion happened.

Where do I cancel a plan or change my card?​

In the billing portal Polar hosts. Polar emails you the link. Billing ends when your account is removed. To stop it sooner, cancel it there.

What if I need help?​

For an ordinary product or legal question, email aoneahsan@gmail.com. Account deletion uses the signed-in page, not email. If the page cannot confirm an outcome, check the account status before you submit again. If that check fails too, write to aoneahsan@gmail.com, and the team can tell you whether a deletion was scheduled; email cannot delete an account.
