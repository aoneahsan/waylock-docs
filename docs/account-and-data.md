# Where can I find account and data information?

<GuideByline path="/guide/account-and-data" />

The [Privacy Policy](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/privacy) explains the information Waylock handles. The [Terms of Service](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/terms) explain the service conditions.

## How would I delete my account after uninstalling the app?

The [account-deletion page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/delete-account) is up, and its flow has not yet passed a live deletion test. It is a signed-in self-service path that asks you to use the Google account linked to Waylock, confirm that sign-in again and explicitly confirm deletion. Deletion then waits 7 days. Sign-in stops during that wait, and you can cancel on the same page or on [Account](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/account). Removal comes on or soon after the date shown, in your time zone. The website and in-app paths need verification; an uncertain response does not prove that deletion happened.

## Where do I cancel a plan or change my card?

In the billing portal Polar hosts. Polar emails you the link. Billing ends when your account is removed. To stop it sooner, cancel it there.

## What if I need help?

For an ordinary product or legal question, email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com). Account deletion uses the signed-in page, not email. If the page cannot confirm an outcome, check the account status before you submit again. If that check fails too, write to [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com), and the team can tell you whether a deletion was scheduled; email cannot delete an account.
