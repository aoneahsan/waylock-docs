# Where can I find account and data information?

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/privacy) for information about Waylock data, the [Terms of Service](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/terms) for service conditions, and [account deletion](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/delete-account) to request removal without reinstalling the app.

For a product or account question, email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com). The public deletion page explains the request and verification process.
