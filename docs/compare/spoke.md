# Moving from Spoke Route Planner to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. A route optimiser for individual delivery drivers, formerly Circuit Route Planner. See <a href="https://spoke.com/route-planner" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Spoke Route Planner</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Spoke Route Planner is the better fit if you want:**

- Unlimited stops a route on Lite, where Waylock Pro plans 15.
- Its pricing page lists first and last stops plainly.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- Hold a stop at a set position inside the order.
- Ask for a stop by distance along the road.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/spoke) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Spoke Route Planner until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
