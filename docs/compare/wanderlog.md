# Moving from Wanderlog to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. A trip itinerary app with routes, stops, lodging and budgets. See <a href="https://wanderlog.com/road-trip-planner" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Wanderlog</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Wanderlog is the better fit if you want:**

- A full itinerary with lodging and budgets.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- Guidance while you drive, with your chosen route kept on the map.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/wanderlog) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Trip planner, set where you start and finish, then ask for a stop by distance along the road.
4. Keep Wanderlog until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
