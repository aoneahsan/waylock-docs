# Moving from Routific to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. Route planning for delivery businesses, priced by orders. See <a href="https://www.routific.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Routific</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Routific is the better fit if you want:**

- Built for a business dispatching many drivers, with capacities and shifts.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- Priced for one driver rather than a delivery business.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/routific) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Routific until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
