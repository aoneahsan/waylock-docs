# Moving from Maposcope to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. A multi-stop route app for couriers, on Apple devices in its App Store listing. See <a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id1502155614" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Maposcope</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Maposcope is the better fit if you want:**

- Up to 500 stops on PRO.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- An Android app.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/maposcope) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Maposcope until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
