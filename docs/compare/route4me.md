# Moving from Route4Me to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. Route planning software for businesses, with a separate driver app. See <a href="https://route4me.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Route4Me</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Route4Me is the better fit if you want:**

- A full web platform for planning across a fleet.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- One plan covers trip planning and delivery order for a driver.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/route4me) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Install the signed APK from the [download page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/download). It needs Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Route4Me until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
