# Moving from Zeo Route Planner to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. A driver app with a fleet dashboard. See <a href="https://zeorouteplanner.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Zeo Route Planner</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Zeo Route Planner is the better fit if you want:**

- A fleet dashboard for running several drivers.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- A plan for one driver with a monthly price on its page.
- A route that stays on the map after a wrong turn.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/zeo) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Install the signed APK from the [download page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/download). It needs Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Zeo Route Planner until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
