# Moving from MyRouteOnline to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. Web route planning paid for by address credits. See <a href="https://www.myrouteonline.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">MyRouteOnline</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**MyRouteOnline is the better fit if you want:**

- Pay-as-you-go credits for occasional large lists.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- An Android navigation screen with your route kept on the map.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/myrouteonline) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep MyRouteOnline until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
