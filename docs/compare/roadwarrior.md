# Moving from RoadWarrior to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. A route planner for individual drivers and teams. See <a href="https://roadwarrior.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">RoadWarrior</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**RoadWarrior is the better fit if you want:**

- Routes up to 120 stops on Pro.
- A team plan is on sale today.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- Stop lock, first, last and before rules.
- A route that stays on the map after a wrong turn.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/roadwarrior) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep RoadWarrior until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
