# Moving from OptimoRoute to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. Route and schedule planning for fleets, priced per driver. See <a href="https://optimoroute.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">OptimoRoute</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**OptimoRoute is the better fit if you want:**

- Hundreds of orders at once across drivers.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- A price made for one driver.
- A trip planner by distance along the road.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/optimoroute) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep OptimoRoute until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
