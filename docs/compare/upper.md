# Moving from Upper Route Planner to Waylock

Checked on 7 November 2026. Route planning for delivery teams, priced per user. See <a href="https://www.upperinc.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Upper Route Planner</a> for its own details.

## How do they differ?

**Upper Route Planner is the better fit if you want:**

- Up to 1,500 stops a route, with proof of delivery.

**Waylock is ahead on:**

- A free plan and a lower price for one driver.

The [full comparison](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/compare/upper) has both sets of prices and every feature row.

## How do I move across?

Waylock has no import from other apps yet, so this is a fresh start.

1. Waylock is in internal testing. Public installation is not available yet.
2. Sign in with Google.
3. On the Delivery planner, add your stops, hold any that must keep their place, and get the order.
4. Keep Upper Route Planner until you have tried a route.

Waylock's controls are built, and their device checks are still open. Google's navigation still does the guiding. [Route controls](/guide/route-controls) explains each one.
