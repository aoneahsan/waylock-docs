# Questions buyers ask about a Waylock licence

<GuideByline path="/guide/white-label/faq" />

## Do I get the source code with a licence?

No. A licence covers your built app and the services we run for it. The code changes hands only with a buyout.

## Can another firm license Waylock as well?

Yes, until somebody buys it outright. A buyout ends sales to anyone else; licences sold before it keep their terms.

## Who publishes the app on Google Play?

You do, under your own developer account. We prepare the build and the listing with you.

## Is there an iOS app?

Not today. Waylock is built for Android. An iOS build is delivered on request when you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## What does a buyout hand over?

The Android app repository, with the Supabase migrations and server functions it runs on, the website repository and the guides repository, plus six months of updates. Google Maps Platform, Google Play, Supabase and payment accounts are opened in your name.

## Can you change Waylock just for my drivers?

Yes, as separate paid work. Describe it on the [custom work page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work) and we reply with questions or a written quote.

## Where are the terms?

The [licence terms](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) explain the licence in plain words. The [Terms of Service](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/terms#white-label) are the binding version and cover payments.
