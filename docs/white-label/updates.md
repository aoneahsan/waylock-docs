# Updates and renewals for a Waylock licence

<GuideByline path="/guide/white-label/updates" />

An update is a new Waylock release applied to your build: fixes, route-control changes and support for newer Android versions.

A licence or a buyout includes six months of updates from the day you pay. A renewal of 3, 6 or 12 months extends them. It counts from the later of today and your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

Without a renewal, your app keeps running on its last version.

Renew on the [updates page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email on the licence. That page shows each renewal's current price.
