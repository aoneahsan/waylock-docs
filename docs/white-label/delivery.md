# What is delivered with a Waylock licence

<GuideByline path="/guide/white-label/delivery" />

Waylock sells one platform group: the mobile app. A licence for it covers four things.

1. **Your Android build.** Your app name, icon and colours, built from the current Waylock release and published under your own Google Play developer account.
2. **The services behind it.** Sign-in, saved stops and routes, and the map and route requests the app makes, set up for your deployment.
3. **Your domain.** The account, plans and account-deletion pages your drivers open from the app, served on the domain you name.
4. **Updates.** New Waylock releases applied to your build for the months included with the licence, then renewals if you want them.

## What about iOS?

Waylock is a native Android app. An iOS app would be a separate build, delivered on request once you provide or fund an Apple developer account. No iOS version exists today.

## What is not included

The source code comes only with a [buyout](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout). Changes made only for your drivers are [custom work](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted separately. Accounts you choose to pay for, such as your domain, your store account and paid services, stay yours.
