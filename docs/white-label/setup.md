# From payment to launch

<GuideByline path="/guide/white-label/setup" />

Access starts when the payment is confirmed to our licensing service, not when you return to the website from the payment page. The licence email carries your key and certificate.

## The order of work

1. **Branding.** We agree your app name, icon, colours and the domain for your drivers' pages by email.
2. **Your build.** We prepare the Android build and the store listing with you.
3. **Your Play listing.** You publish the app under your Google Play developer account. Google reviews it on its own schedule.
4. **Deployment.** We deploy the services and pages on your domain, with your licence file at `/.well-known/waylock-white-label-license.json`.

No launch date is promised here: Play review and your own decisions set the pace. Ask on the [custom work page](https://waylock.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work) if you need a date agreed in writing.
